h3-transfer

Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026

h3-transfer is a personal file transfer utility operated by Siyuan Yang. This policy describes the Google account data used by its Drive integration.

Data access and purpose

The app requests the drive.file permission to create, read, update, and delete files created by the app or explicitly made available to it. File contents and metadata, such as names, sizes, identifiers, and checksums, are used to perform and verify transfers between computers configured by the operator.

Storage and recipients

Files pass through the authorized Google Drive account and the source and destination computers selected by the operator. OAuth tokens are stored in the rclone configuration on computers where the operator configures this remote. Transfer logs may contain filenames, file identifiers, timestamps, sizes, and error information.

The Drive integration's purpose is file transfer. It does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. Google processes data under its own privacy policy. Access on each participating computer is governed by that computer's access controls.

Retention and deletion

Transfer files remain in Drive until the operator or a configured cleanup process removes them. Local copies and logs may remain on participating computers until removed there. Deleting a Drive copy does not automatically delete copies on other computers.

Your controls

You can revoke the app's access in your Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorized access but does not itself delete existing files or local copies. You can delete Drive files through Google Drive and remove local configuration, files, and logs from participating computers.

This website

These information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They contain no analytics scripts, sign-in form, or file upload form. GitHub may process ordinary request information to operate the hosting service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Contact and updates

Contact siyuan.reed@gmail.com with questions about h3-transfer or its data practices. Changes to this policy will appear on this page with an updated effective date.
